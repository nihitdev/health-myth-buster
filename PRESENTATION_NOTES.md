# Presentation notes

The official six-question PPT template was not supplied. The six prompts below are a suggested mapping; copy content into the actual template only after confirming its questions. Do not claim an official question wording.

1. **What problem did we choose?** Health tips spread through chats and forwards. Students and families need a simple way to pause and inspect reliable evidence.
2. **Who benefits?** Indian students, families, teachers and NGO volunteers seeking general health literacy. The prototype supports English input; it cannot give personal medical advice.
3. **What did we build?** Health Myth Buster: a free claim checker, searchable source library, transparent methodology, local testing lab and presentation route. It returns TRUE, FALSE or UNVERIFIED and ends answers with “Always ask a doctor.”
4. **How does it work and fit the theme?** Text normalization and conservative keyword rules search a local database. No LLM or machine learning is used. Responsible digital futures require evidence, transparency and knowing when to abstain. We do not call the current prototype autonomous AI.

6. **What is the impact and next step?** Easier access to evidence, with no account or paid API. Currently 36 reviewed entries and 31 pending. Priorities: independent medical review, real user evaluation, expanding verified Indian-source coverage, and safely testing multilingual support.

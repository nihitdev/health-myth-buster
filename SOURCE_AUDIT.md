# Source audit

Evidence content reviewed during development on 2026-10-22. This is an editorial source-content check, not independent clinical certification. **36 evidence-reviewed entries; 31 pending; target 50.** No human evaluation outcomes are claimed.

## Review method

Official page content was opened and read using the browsing tool. Each published claim has at least two relevant official pages. Gutkha wording additionally uses MoHFW NTCP and NCI content directly identifying this Indian smokeless-tobacco product. Some pairs are two distinct CDC documents rather than two different organizations. ANACS is an Indian government AIDS-control authority. Negated variants are separately reviewed phrasings and do not inflate the entry count. Review notes below identify the supporting sections; titles and URLs come from the opened pages. A successful HTTP response alone is not evidence. The NHS page reports a 2022 source review date; this is older guidance and should receive a team recheck. Development review date is separate from publisher dates.

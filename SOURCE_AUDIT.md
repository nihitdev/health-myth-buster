## Reviewed entries

### ant-cold: Antibiotics cure the common cold.

- Verdict: FALSE; category: Medicines; status: evidence-reviewed; reviewed: 2026-10-22.
- Reason: Colds are caused by viruses. Antibiotics act against bacteria, so they do not cure a cold.
- [CDC: Healthy Habits: Antibiotic Do’s and Don’ts](https://www.cdc.gov/antibiotic-use/about/index.html) — Sections on viruses, prescribing, sharing medicines and side effects.
- [NHS: Overview — Antibiotics](https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/antibiotics/) — Overview, how to take antibiotics, side effects and considerations.

### ant-flu: Antibiotics cure the flu.

- Verdict: FALSE; category: Medicines; status: evidence-reviewed; reviewed: 2026-10-22.
